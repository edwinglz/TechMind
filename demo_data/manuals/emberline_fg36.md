# Emberline Kitchen FG-36 Gas Flat-Top Griddle: Service Manual

Model FG-36. 36 in. countertop gas griddle, 1 in. steel plate, three burners with independent thermostats and spark ignition. Rev. 2.0.

## System overview

### Gas system

Gas flows from the manual shut-off through the pressure regulator to three thermostat-controlled burners. Each burner has its own orifice (P-ORIF) and spark igniter (P-IGNT).

### Plate and burners

Each burner heats a 12 in. zone of the plate. A partly blocked orifice gives a small, yellow flame and a cold zone.

### Temperature control

Each zone has a snap-action thermostat (P-GTSTAT) with a capillary bulb under the plate. Dial range 200-450 degrees F.

## Installation and specifications

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Gas supply pressure (natural gas) | 7 in. water column |
| Manifold pressure | 4 in. water column |
| Dial range | 200-450 degrees F |
| Clearance to combustibles | 6 in. sides, 6 in. back |

## Cleaning and maintenance

**Daily (store staff):** scrape the plate, empty the grease drawer, season the plate.

**Monthly (technician):** check flame on every burner; clean orifices if the flame is yellow or lifting.

**Quarterly (technician):** calibrate thermostats with a surface thermometer.

## Troubleshooting

### Uneven heating

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| One zone cool, small or yellow flame | Clogged orifice | Clean or replace orifice (P-ORIF) |
| Whole plate cool | Low gas pressure | Check supply and manifold pressure |

### Burner won't light

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Clicking, no flame | Igniter cracked or misaligned | Replace igniter (P-IGNT) |
| No clicking | No power to igniter module | Check plug and breaker |

### Wrong temperature

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Plate much hotter or cooler than dial | Thermostat out of calibration | Calibrate; replace thermostat (P-GTSTAT) |

## Safety

- **Gas leaks.** If anyone smells gas, don't operate switches or flames, get everyone out, and call the gas company.
- **Burns.** The plate stays hot for over an hour after shut-off.
- **Grease fires.** Empty the grease drawer daily. Never use water on a grease fire.
