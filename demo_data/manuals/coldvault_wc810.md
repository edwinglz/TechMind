# Coldvault WC-810 Walk-In Cooler System: Service Manual

Model WC-810. 8 x 10 ft walk-in cooler with ceiling-mounted evaporator and outdoor air-cooled condensing unit. Rev. 1.6.

## System overview

### Box and door

Insulated panels, self-closing door with magnetic gasket (P-GSKT), strip curtain, and door heater wire.

### Evaporator (inside)

The evaporator coil with fan motors (P-EVFAN) cools the air inside the box. A defrost timer (P-DFTMR) stops cooling four times a day so frost melts off the coil. If defrost fails, ice builds up, airflow drops, and the box warms.

### Condensing unit (outside)

Compressor, condenser coil, condenser fan motor (P-CDFAN) with run capacitor (P-CAP), and contactor (P-CONT). A failed condenser fan makes the unit overheat and shut off on its high-pressure safety, mostly on hot days.

## Installation and specifications

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Box temperature | 35-38 degrees F (food safety limit 41 degrees F) |
| Defrost | 4 per day, 30 minutes each |
| Refrigerant | R-448A |
| Power | 208-230 V condensing unit; 115 V evaporator |

## Cleaning and maintenance

**Daily (store staff):** log box temperature twice; keep the door closed; keep boxes 6 in. away from the evaporator.

**Monthly (store staff):** wipe the door gasket and check it seals.

**Quarterly (technician):** clean the condenser coil, check refrigerant charge and amperage, test defrost.

## Troubleshooting

### Box warm

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Ice on evaporator coil, weak airflow | Defrost timer failed | Manual defrost; replace timer (P-DFTMR) |
| Door ajar or frost at the frame | Door blocked or not latching | Clear the door; check latch and gasket |
| Outdoor unit silent or loud, condenser hot | Condenser fan or capacitor failed | Replace fan (P-CDFAN) or capacitor (P-CAP) |
| Everything running, box still warm | Low refrigerant | Leak-check and recharge (P-RFRG) |

### Water on the floor

| Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|
| Evaporator drain line frozen or clogged | Clear and heat the drain line |

## Safety

- **Food safety.** Above 41 degrees F, food is at risk. Move it to another cooler, log temperatures, and follow the food safety policy.
- **Trapped inside.** The door has an inside release; never block it.
- **Electrical.** Turn off power at the disconnect before touching fans or the condensing unit.
- **Refrigerant** is handled only by certified technicians.
