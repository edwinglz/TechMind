# Glacier Peak Dispense DI-8 Drop-In Dispenser: Service Manual

Model DI-8. Drop-in post-mix beverage dispenser that mounts into a counter cut-out above an ice bin with a cast cold plate. Eight valves. Carbonated water is supplied by a remote carbonator. Rev. 2.4.

## System overview

### Water supply

Plain water and carbonated water arrive from the remote carbonator through the python (insulated line bundle). The DI-8 has no pump of its own.

### CO2 and regulators

CO2 from a cylinder in the back room feeds the remote carbonator through its primary regulator and the bag-in-box pumps through a secondary regulator (P-REG2).

### Carbonator pump

The carbonator pump is part of the remote carbonator, not the DI-8. See the remote carbonator manual for pump service. Flat drinks on all eight valves usually point to the carbonator or the CO2 supply.

### Bag-in-box syrup pumps

One gas-driven BIB pump per flavor on the syrup rack, connected with quick-disconnect connectors (P-BIBC).

### Cold plate and ice bin

Beverage lines run through a cold plate cast into the floor of the drop-in ice bin. Staff fill the bin with ice from the ice machine. Drinks are only cold while ice sits on the plate.

### Valves and nozzles

Eight post-mix valves with removable nozzles and diffusers (P-NOZ), sealed with valve seal and O-ring kits (P-VSEAL), opened by 24 V solenoids (P-SOL).

## Installation and pressures

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Carbonated water supply | from remote carbonator, 90-100 psi CO2 |
| CO2 secondary regulator (BIB pumps) | 60-70 psi |
| Target drink temperature | 40 degrees F or colder |
| Power | 24 V transformer, 115 V input |

The ice bin drain must slope to an open drain with an air gap.

## Brix calibration

Standard ratio 5:1 water to syrup.

1. Remove the nozzle and diffuser.
2. Fit the syrup separator and pour into a brix cup for 4 seconds.
3. Adjust the syrup flow control on the valve body until the levels match the ratio.
4. Refit the nozzle and taste-test.

## Daily and weekly cleaning

**Daily (store staff):** soak nozzles and diffusers in warm sanitizer water for 10 minutes; wipe valve undersides; empty the drip tray.

**Weekly (store staff):** check the ice bin drain flows; keep the bin at least half full of ice during service.

**Quarterly (technician):** empty and sanitize the ice bin, check pressures, inspect the drain tube.

## Troubleshooting

### No carbonation (flat drinks)

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Flat on all valves, cylinder gauge in red | CO2 cylinder empty | Swap cylinder |
| Flat on all valves, gauge normal | Cylinder valve closed | Open cylinder valve |
| Flat on all valves, CO2 OK | Remote carbonator fault | See remote carbonator manual |

### Watery or syrupy drinks

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| One flavor clear | Bag-in-box empty or disconnected | Replace bag, reconnect |
| One flavor off-taste, bag OK | Brix drifted | Recalibrate |

### No flow on one valve

Clogged nozzle (clean it) or failed solenoid (replace P-SOL).

### No flow on all valves

Check the 24 V transformer is plugged in, the water supply is open, and the remote carbonator is powered.

### Foaming

Usually warm drinks or a dirty nozzle. Check ice on the cold plate and clean nozzles.

### Warm drinks

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Ice bin low | Not enough ice | Fill the bin |
| Ice fused into one block | Ice bridge above the cold plate (common in summer) | Break up and remove the bridge |
| Water pooling in the bin | Drain clogged or tube cracked | Clear drain; replace drain tube (P-DRAIN) |

### Leaks

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Nozzle drips after pouring | Worn valve seal or O-ring | Replace P-VSEAL kit |
| Water under the counter | Drain tube cracked | Replace P-DRAIN |

### No ice

The DI-8 does not make or dispense ice. Staff fill the bin from the ice machine.

## Safety

- **CO2 asphyxiation:** CO2 collects in low, enclosed spaces. If a leak is suspected, ventilate and leave the area.
- **High-pressure gas:** never loosen pressurized CO2 fittings.
- **Electrical:** unplug the transformer before working near wet areas. Panels are for technicians only.
- **Sanitation:** mold in nozzles and a dirty ice bin are a health hazard; sanitize before serving.
