# Emberline Kitchen EF-40 Gas Fryer: Service Manual

Model EF-40. 40 lb oil capacity, tube-type natural gas fryer with standing pilot, electronic thermostat, and built-in filtration. Rev. 4.1.

## System overview

### Gas system

Natural gas enters through the manual shut-off valve and the gas control valve (P-GASV). The gas control valve has a safety feature: it only stays open while the pilot thermocouple (P-THCPL) is hot. If the pilot goes out, the thermocouple cools within about 30 seconds and the valve shuts off all gas.

### Burners and heat exchange tubes

Four burners fire into heat exchange tubes that pass through the oil. Exhaust leaves through the flue at the back.

### Temperature control

A thermostat probe (P-FTSTAT) in the oil reports temperature to the controller, which opens and closes the main burner gas. A separate high-limit switch (P-HILIM) cuts all gas if the oil passes 450 degrees F. The high-limit does not reset until the oil cools.

### Power

The controller and electronic display need 120 V power. Without power, the fryer will not heat, even with gas on.

## Installation and specifications

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Gas supply pressure (natural gas) | 7 in. water column |
| Manifold pressure | 3.5 in. water column |
| Oil capacity | 40 lb |
| Operating range | 200-375 degrees F |
| High-limit trip | 450 degrees F |
| Power | 120 V, 2 A |

Restrain the fryer with the supplied cable so it can't move away from the gas connector.

## Cleaning and maintenance

**Daily (store staff):** filter the oil using the built-in filter routine; wipe the cabinet; check the oil level.

**Weekly (store staff):** boil-out with fryer cleaner per the cleaning card.

**Quarterly (technician):** check gas pressures, calibrate the thermostat probe, inspect the thermocouple and pilot flame, test the high-limit.

## Troubleshooting

### Will not heat

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Display dark | Fryer unplugged or breaker tripped | Check the plug; reset breaker once |
| Pilot goes out when the knob is released | Thermocouple worn | Replace thermocouple (P-THCPL) |
| Pilot lit, burners won't fire | Gas control valve failed | Replace gas control valve (P-GASV) |
| Shut off after smoking oil | High-limit tripped | Let cool; test and replace high-limit (P-HILIM); check thermostat |

### Slow recovery or wrong temperature

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Oil slow to heat, soggy product | Probe reads low | Calibrate or replace probe (P-FTSTAT) |
| Oil smoking | Probe stuck or controller fault | Turn off; replace probe; check high-limit |
| Uneven heating | Carbon on burners | Clean burners and tubes |

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | First action |
|---|---|---|
| HOT-HI | Oil above 410 degrees F | Turn off; technician to check probe |
| PROBE | Probe open or shorted | Technician: replace probe |
| IGN | Ignition failure | Check gas is on; technician: check pilot and valve |

## Safety

- **Hot oil.** Oil at 350 degrees F causes severe burns. Let the fryer cool before any service. Never put water on an oil fire; use the hood suppression system or a Class K extinguisher.
- **Gas leaks.** If anyone smells gas, don't operate switches or flames, get everyone out, and call the gas company.
- **Relighting the pilot** is done only by trained staff or technicians following the lighting instructions on the door.
