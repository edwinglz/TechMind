# Fizzwell Systems RC-100 Remote Carbonator: Service Manual

Model RC-100. Back-room remote carbonator that supplies carbonated water to one or more drop-in or countertop dispensers. Rev. 1.8.

## System overview

### Water supply

Incoming water passes a water filter cartridge (P-WFILT) and the pump inlet strainer (P-STRN) before reaching the carbonator pump.

### CO2 and regulators

The CO2 cylinder's primary regulator feeds the carbonator tank at 90-100 psi. A secondary regulator (P-REG2) for the syrup pumps is usually mounted on the same board.

### Carbonator pump

A rotary vane pump with a 1/3 hp motor (P-CPUMP) fills the stainless carbonator tank. A check valve (P-CHKV) at the pump inlet prevents backflow. A level probe starts and stops the pump. A relief valve (P-RELV) on the tank opens above 150 psi.

### Bag-in-box syrup pumps

Not part of the RC-100; see the dispenser manual.

### Cold plate and ice bin

Not part of the RC-100. Carbonated water is cooled at the dispenser's cold plate.

### Valves and nozzles

Not part of the RC-100; see the dispenser manual.

## Installation and pressures

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Incoming water pressure | 40-50 psi dynamic, minimum 25 psi |
| CO2 primary regulator | 90-100 psi |
| Relief valve opening pressure | 150 psi |
| Pump run time per fill | under 60 seconds |
| Power | 115 V, dedicated 15 A circuit |

Mount on a wall in a ventilated room. Chain the CO2 cylinder upright.

## Brix calibration

Brix is set at the dispenser valves; see the dispenser manual. Low carbonator output makes every flavor taste watery, so fix carbonator problems before calibrating brix.

## Daily and weekly cleaning

**Weekly (store staff):** check the CO2 cylinder gauge and that the cylinder valve is open.

**Quarterly (technician):** replace the water filter cartridge, clean the inlet strainer, check pressures and pump run time.

## Troubleshooting

### No carbonation (flat drinks)

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Gauge in red | CO2 cylinder empty | Swap cylinder |
| Gauge normal, flat everywhere | Cylinder valve closed | Open the valve |
| Pump runs nonstop, motor hot | Pump worn, not reaching pressure | Replace pump motor assembly (P-CPUMP) |
| Pump cycles rapidly | Low water pressure or clogged filter or strainer | Replace filter (P-WFILT); clean strainer (P-STRN) |

### Watery or syrupy drinks

All flavors watery and pouring slowly usually means low water flow: check filter and inlet pressure.

### No flow on one valve

Dispenser-side problem; see the dispenser manual.

### No flow on all valves

Check RC-100 power, the water supply valve, and the filter cartridge.

### Foaming

Primary CO2 pressure too high; set to 90-100 psi.

### Warm drinks

Not caused by the RC-100; check the dispenser's ice and cold plate.

### Leaks

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Spraying or dripping from top of tank, hissing | Relief valve worn or tank over-pressured | Check primary regulator; replace relief valve (P-RELV) |
| Drip at pump fittings | Loose fitting | Depressurize, then tighten |

### No ice

Not applicable to the RC-100.

## Safety

- **CO2 asphyxiation:** the RC-100 is usually installed in a back room. CO2 collects in low, enclosed spaces. If a leak is suspected (hissing, headache, dizziness), ventilate and leave the area.
- **High-pressure gas:** the carbonator tank runs at up to 100 psi. Close the CO2 valve and relieve pressure before servicing.
- **Electrical:** unplug before opening the cover; keep water away from the motor.
