# Polar Crest IM-500 Air-Cooled Ice Machine: Service Manual

Model IM-500. Modular cube ice machine, up to 500 lb per day, air-cooled condenser, mounted on a storage bin. Rev. 3.3.

## System overview

### Water system

Water enters through the water inlet valve (P-IMWIV), fills the trough, and is pumped over the evaporator plate. A filter cartridge (P-WFILT) upstream of the machine is recommended.

### Refrigeration

A compressor and air-cooled condenser chill the evaporator. The condenser draws air through a removable front air filter (P-IMAF). A clogged filter makes the condenser run hot and ice production falls sharply.

### Freeze and harvest cycle

Ice forms on the evaporator during the freeze cycle (about 12-15 minutes). Hot gas then releases the slab, which breaks into cubes and drops into the bin. A bin-level sensor stops production when the bin is full.

## Installation and specifications

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Water pressure | 20-80 psi |
| Ambient air temperature | 50-100 degrees F |
| Freeze cycle | 12-15 minutes at 70 degrees F air |
| Clearance | 6 in. at sides and back for airflow |
| Power | 115 V, dedicated 20 A circuit |

Production drops about 20% for every 10 degrees F the room is above 70 degrees F, so summer output is always lower.

## Cleaning and maintenance

**Monthly (store staff):** pull out the front air filter (no tools), rinse in warm soapy water, dry, and slide it back.

**Every 6 months (technician):** descale the water system with scale remover (P-IMSCL), sanitize the bin and trough, clean the condenser coil.

## Troubleshooting

### Low ice production

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Warm air at front grille, filter dusty | Clogged air filter | Rinse or replace the filter (P-IMAF) |
| Small or cloudy cubes, long freeze, E-3 | Scale buildup | Descale (P-IMSCL); clean condenser |
| Room very hot | High ambient temperature | Improve ventilation |

### No ice

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Trough empty, E-1 | No water: supply off or inlet valve failed | Open supply; clean screen; replace valve (P-IMWIV) |
| Machine dark | No power | Check plug and breaker |
| Bin full light on, bin not full | Bin sensor blocked | Clean the sensor |

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | First action |
|---|---|---|
| E-1 | No water detected | Check water supply and inlet valve |
| E-2 | Long harvest | Technician: check hot gas valve |
| E-3 | Long freeze | Clean air filter; descale; check condenser |
| E-5 | High condenser temperature | Clean air filter and condenser |

## Safety

- **Sanitation.** Mold and slime in the bin or on the evaporator contaminate ice. Ice is food: sanitize on schedule.
- **Electrical.** Unplug before removing panels. Panels are for technicians only.
- **Water near electrical parts.** Wipe up leaks before touching the machine.
