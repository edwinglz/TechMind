# Airmark Climate RT-5 Rooftop HVAC Unit: Service Manual

Model RT-5. 5-ton packaged rooftop unit with gas heat and electric cooling, controlled by a wall thermostat. Rev. 2.7.

## System overview

### Airflow

The indoor blower pulls air through the return-air grille and filter (P-HVFLT) and pushes it across the evaporator coil and gas heat exchanger to the supply ducts. A clogged filter cuts airflow and comfort, and can freeze the coil.

### Cooling

The compressor is started by a contactor (P-CONT) and needs a run capacitor (P-CAP). The outdoor condenser fan (P-CDFAN) removes heat. Capacitors and fan motors fail most often during summer heat.

### Thermostat

A battery-backed wall thermostat calls for heating or cooling. With dead batteries or no power, the screen goes blank and nothing runs.

## Installation and specifications

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Cooling capacity | 5 tons |
| Supply air in cooling | 15-20 degrees F colder than return air |
| Filter | 20 x 25 x 2 in., replace monthly in summer |
| Thermostat batteries | 2 x AA |
| Power | 208-230 V, rooftop disconnect |

## Cleaning and maintenance

**Monthly (store staff):** replace the return-air filter (grille latches open, no tools).

**Spring and fall (technician):** clean coils, check refrigerant charge, test capacitor and contactor, check belts, inspect heat exchanger.

## Troubleshooting

### Not cooling

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Weak airflow, dirty filter | Clogged return filter | Replace filter (P-HVFLT) |
| Outdoor unit hums, compressor won't start | Failed run capacitor | Replace capacitor (P-CAP) |
| Compressor runs, warm air | Low refrigerant | Leak-check and recharge (P-RFRG) |
| Condenser fan not spinning, grinding noise | Fan motor failed | Replace fan motor (P-CDFAN) and capacitor |

### Nothing runs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Thermostat blank | Dead batteries or breaker tripped | Replace batteries; reset breaker once |
| Thermostat on, unit silent | Contactor failed | Replace contactor (P-CONT) |

## Safety

- **Roof access and falls.** Only technicians go on the roof, using the fixed ladder and fall protection.
- **Electrical.** Turn off the rooftop disconnect before opening panels.
- **Gas heat.** If anyone smells gas, get everyone out and call the gas company.
- **Refrigerant** is handled only by certified technicians.
